These go with the Mathematical Methods 3 & 4 Bound Reference by Harry Nguyen. They work on any phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install and no account.

03 · Trigonometry

03Symmetry with ASTCDrag an angle round the unit circle and see its three reflections, their signs and exact values. 03Sketching sine and cosineChange a, n, θ and b and watch one cycle and its five key points move.

04 · Transformations

04Transformations of y = x²Change a, h and k in y = a(x − h)² + k and watch the image move away from the parent.

05 · Calculus

05The derivative graphDrag a point along f and watch the gradient of its tangent trace out f′. 05Estimating area under a graphLeft endpoint, right endpoint and trapezium estimates against the exact area.

06 · Probability

06The binomial distributionChange n and p and read off any binomial probability, with the TI-Nspire command. 06The normal distributionShade a probability, or find k with invNorm, and see the z scale underneath. 06What a confidence interval meansTake hundreds of samples and count how many intervals catch the true p.
